From today's featured article

The Jacquard loom is a device fitted to a loom that simplifies the process of manufacturing textiles with complex patterns. It was invented by Joseph Marie Jacquard and first shown in 1804.

The loom was controlled by a chain of punched cards laced together, an idea later taken up by Charles Babbage for his Analytical Engine.
