Alan Turing

Alan Mathison Turing (23 June 1912 - 7 June 1954) was an English mathematician, computer scientist and logician.

He was born in Maida Vale, London, and studied at King's College, Cambridge.

During the Second World War, Turing worked at Bletchley Park on breaking German ciphers.

Winston Churchill later called the codebreakers the geese that laid the golden eggs.
