Leaders meet in Washington

By Priya Nair, New Delhi

Updated 30 Sep 2026, 10:42 IST

Prime Minister Narendra Modi met President Joe Biden in Washington on Tuesday to discuss trade.

Markets rose on the news: the Sensex closed at 81,234.56, up 1.2 per cent.

Travellers can call the embassy helpline on 1800-11-4000.
